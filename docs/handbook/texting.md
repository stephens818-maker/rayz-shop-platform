# Texting a client

1. Texts stay off until the client agrees. A number that replied STOP cannot be texted until it replies START.
2. On the calendar, open the appointment and open Messages to the client. You see the thread, not the phone number.
3. You can answer a client who texted the shop in the last day, even before they opt in to marketing texts.
4. The sandbox does not send a real text. The message sits in the outbox.
